Payroll Desk - period-number control and review highlights

INSTALL
Save the current company once and optionally export a backup. Extract the ZIP and replace app.js, index.html and sw.js in the main folder of Payroll-desk1. After GitHub Pages deployment finishes, reload the website in Safari or close/reopen the installed app.

USE
For the 24-period schedule, choose Payroll year and then Pay period number 1–24. Period 1 is January 1–15; period 2 is January 16–31; period 18 is September 16–30; period 19 is October 1–15; period 24 is December 16–31. February handles leap years.
Changing the period updates its start/end, banking-calendar payday and processing deadline, statement date and internal advice number. A previously saved advice number for the employee/period is reused. Employee file and clock numbers stay stable.
Calculated YTD follows the selected period. To use this for repeated constant-pay projections, select the calculated YTD source once. Manually entered YTD is stored with its specific period; new periods need their own entered totals. The app does not silently change the YTD source.

REVIEW HIGHLIGHTS
Blue outlines show automatically updated fields. Amber outlines identify items to review: employee file/clock number, PTO/sick balances, contributions, deposit account, entered totals and payment details. Earlier periods also highlight salary, W-4 settings and employee address. These are nonblocking reminders; Clear review highlights removes them after review. They are never drawn on the PDF.
When changing to a previously unsaved period, the old period's actual-payment confirmation is cleared. The existing final-statement payment validation remains unchanged. Returning to a saved period restores its entered balances, overrides and confirmation fields.
The existing company profile stores period snapshots, identifiers and review state; backups include them. Autosave continues to reopen the last company and selected period.

SPACING
In the classic template, the employee name-to-address baseline gap is now 16 points, matching the first-row spacing in the employer block. Address rows continue at 14-point spacing. The employer phone remains its own row.

PDF EXPORT
Exports are newly created PDFs, rather than edits to previous files. The actual export time is recorded in CreationDate and ModDate. Creator and Producer remain the actual software identity. No review highlighting or new notices are added to PDF pages. Existing footer removal, embedded fonts, address parsing and device-neutral metadata remain included.
