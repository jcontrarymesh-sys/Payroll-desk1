Company workspace

Company profiles and form entries save automatically in this browser. The last selected company reopens with its saved entries. Do not enter complete SSNs, account numbers, or live confidential payroll information until encrypted storage is implemented. Back up regularly.

Company benefit defaults

Regular profiles start with the Adelaide policy: the PTO grant is available once probation is completed. Company holidays are separate. Startup / owner-manager-only profiles have these benefit defaults switched off. Policy settings do not change an employee's recorded PTO balance. Changes save automatically on this device.

Comments

Statement dates & PDF metadata

The PDF Author uses the employer legal name; Creator and Producer identify the actual app and PDF engine. Fonts are embedded. Creation and modification timestamps reflect the export time. The PDF creation timestamp is recorded automatically when the file is generated; statement and pay dates are separate.

Generate statement

PDFs contain selectable text and honest creation metadata. Draft PDFs are labeled DRAFT. Final PDFs require actual payment verification and are not digitally signed or authenticated by a payroll provider.

All calculations run locally in your browser. This edition does not upload employee information. Local browser storage is not suitable for sensitive live payroll records. For real HR use, secure your device and verify calculations independently.
